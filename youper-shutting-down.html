Youper is shutting down on 30 September 2026

If you have been journalling in it, the thing that matters is the export. Everything else can wait.

The deadline

Youper's own site carries the notice: “Youper is shutting down September 30, 2026 — export your data.” The company says accounts, chat histories and personal data are permanently deleted from its servers and backups from 1 October.

So there is a window, and after it your entries are not recoverable by anyone, including Youper.

How to export

Youper publishes an export page at start.youper.ai/export-data, which is where to go if you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in. It was loading when this page was written on 25 August 2026.

If you still have the app, the route reported elsewhere is Settings → Request My Data. I have not been able to verify that one myself, so treat the web export as the reliable route and the in-app path as worth trying first if you have it open.

Do it before you decide anything else. Choosing a new app can wait five weeks; the export cannot.

One thing that makes this easy to miss

Youper was already removed from the App Store earlier in 2026, which means it does not come up in a search. If it is still installed on your phone it keeps working for now, and nothing obliges you to open it between now and the deadline.

That is the gap this page exists for: the people most likely to lose their writing are the ones who stopped using it a while ago and have no reason to look.

If you want somewhere else to keep a thought record

You do not need an app. A thought record is a page with columns on it, and the format is here, free, with nothing to sign up for.

If you would rather have an app, these were all present and maintained when last checked — dates and method on the checked list:

  • Clarity: CBT Self Help Journal — Inquiry Health
  • MindShift CBT — free, from Anxiety Canada's programme
  • Untangle: CBT Companion — Resiliens
  • Wysa — if the chat format was the part you valued
  • Relief — mine. Listed here for the same reason as the others and with the same disclosure I put everywhere: it is a CBT thought record for iPhone, five complete entries per quarter free, and I would rather you exported your Youper data than downloaded anything.

Nothing on this page depends on you choosing any of them.
