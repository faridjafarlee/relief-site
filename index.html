What Relief does

Relief is a private, science-backed Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) companion. When a thought is spinning you out, it walks you through the same step-by-step process therapists use: capture the thought, spot the cognitive distortion, weigh the evidence, write a balanced reframe, and watch your distress drop — measured before and after.

  • Truly private — everything stays on your device, encrypted. No account, no cloud.
  • Works offline, anytime.
  • Built on real CBT techniques and a library of common thinking traps.
  • Pattern insights that show how your mind works over time.
