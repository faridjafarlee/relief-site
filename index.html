What Relief does

Relief is a private, CBT-based companion for untangling anxious, stressful, and self-critical thoughts. When a thought is spinning you out, it walks you through the step-by-step reframing process used in cognitive behavioral therapy: capture the automatic thought, spot the cognitive distortion behind it, weigh the evidence for and against, write a balanced reframe, then rate your distress again and see what actually changed.

  • Truly private — everything stays on your device, encrypted. No account, no cloud.
  • Works offline, anytime.
  • Built on real CBT techniques and a library of common thinking traps.
  • Pattern insights that show how your mind works over time.
