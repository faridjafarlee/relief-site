Relief

For therapists

If you are deciding whether this is worth mentioning to a client, this page is the short factual version. No claims about outcomes, because the app cannot support them.

What it is

A single-purpose thought record on iPhone. The client works through one automatic thought at a time, in this order:

  • Write the automatic thought, and rate how distressing it feels
  • Identify the cognitive distortion behind it, from a fixed set of ten
  • List evidence for and evidence against
  • Write a more balanced version of the thought
  • Rate the distress again

The second rating is optional and is only recorded if the client confirms it. It is not assumed to go down, and it may go up.

What it deliberately is not

  • Not an AI chatbot. There is no language model anywhere in the app. The client writes their own reframe; the app supplies the structure and nothing else.
  • Not a mood tracker or a meditation app. It does one technique.
  • Not a treatment, and not a substitute for one. It makes no claim to reduce symptoms and is not a medical device.
  • Not a monitoring tool. There is no clinician dashboard and no way for anyone, including us, to see a client's entries.

Where the client's writing goes

Nowhere. Entries are stored in a database on the device and the free-text fields — the thought, the evidence, the reframe, the takeaway — are encrypted with AES-GCM, with the key held in the device keychain. There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud sync and no server to hold anything. The app works offline.

The app can be locked behind a passcode or Face ID, which matters if a client shares a device.

When a client wants it gone

“Delete all data” removes the entries, the edit history, the attached photos and voice notes, and the diagnostics log. It also rewrites the database file itself, so the deleted text is not merely unlinked and left recoverable in freed pages.

This matters more for some clients than for others. A shared or handed-on device, or a situation where someone else may go looking, is the case it was built for.

Bringing the work to session

The client can export their full history as JSON at any time, on any plan, and show or send it however they choose. Since nothing syncs anywhere, an export is the only way records leave the phone — which means the client decides, every time, what you see.

What it costs the client

Five complete reframes every three months are free, with no account and no payment card. A subscription removes that limit and unlocks summaries of recurring distortions.

The personal safety plan, the crisis resources, the app lock and the full data export are free on every plan and are never placed behind the subscription.

Honest limitations

  • iPhone only. There is no Android or web version.
  • Because nothing is backed up to a server, a lost phone means lost entries unless the client has exported. Worth saying out loud when you mention it.
  • Ten distortions, not an exhaustive taxonomy.
  • The app is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Ukrainian, Japanese and Korean.
